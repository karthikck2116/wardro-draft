# Product model

Wardrobes are Shopify products. Colour and Mirror are variants when supported by that product. Accessories are separate sellable products/variants and separate cart lines. Product-specific specifications live in the `wardro` metafield namespace. The storefront must show no option or specification that Shopify does not supply.

Cart attributes include `delivery_pincode`, `preferred_installation_date`, `installation_required`, and `customised_order_acknowledged`. The preferred date is subject to confirmation and must be after estimated delivery.
