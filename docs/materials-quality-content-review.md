# Materials & Quality content review

The public Materials & Quality page intentionally uses neutral, model-specific
wording until Wardro supplies approved business specifications or Shopify
metafields.

The following claims are **not shown** and require business verification before
publication:

- panel thicknesses that are not confirmed for every model;
- moisture, termite or borer resistance;
- hinge or channel cycle-test figures;
- load-bearing figures;
- hardware brand names;
- laboratory tests or certifications.

Editable page content is centralised in `data/materials-quality.ts`. When
approved data is available, prefer product metafields for details that vary by
model and keep the page-level wording accurate for the full range.
