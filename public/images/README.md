# Wardro image library

Keep imagery organised by purpose. Product and category assets must show the complete wardrobe clearly; lifestyle images are reserved for editorial content.

- `home/`: hero images, ideally 1800 × 1200 px or larger.
- `categories/`: category wardrobe images, ideally 900 × 900 px.
- `products/`: catalogue images, ideally 1000 × 1200 or 1200 × 1200 px. Use `{handle}-{view}.webp` when multiple views exist.
- `materials/`: board and hardware details, ideally 900 × 600 px.
- `guides/`: editorial article images, ideally 1200 × 800 px.

Use lowercase kebab-case names, no embedded copy or logos, accurate alt text in code, sRGB colour, and WebP/AVIF for final production uploads where practical.
